# KEYWORDS — finding aid for Nossa Senhora das Graças de Cimbres

Naming variants for searching corpora, archives and the web. **Listing a term
is not asserting it** — this file is a finding aid (see the sourcing-rules
skill: absence is a claim and needs its own evidence). Record here any term
that reliably returns nothing, with the corpus and date checked.

## The apparition / the devotion

- pt: Nossa Senhora das Graças de Cimbres; Nossa Senhora da Graça (the 2021
  pastoral letter's wording); Nossa Senhora de Cimbres; Aparições de Cimbres;
  Aparição de Cimbres; "Eu sou a graça" (the reported self-identification —
  distinctive search phrase); Virgem de Cimbres
- es: Nuestra Señora de las Gracias de Cimbres; Nuestra Señora de la Gracia;
  apariciones de Cimbres; Virgen de Cimbres
- fr: Notre-Dame des Grâces de Cimbres; apparitions de Cimbres

Miraculous Medal devotion (Rue du Bac, Paris, 1830); always pair with

## Auto-caption manglings (18 devotional videos, vaulted 2026-08)

Written as prose deliberately: `corpus-index.py` turns two-column table rows in
this file into query expansions, and feeding it ASR garbage would poison every
later search. These are what the machine heard, recorded so a reader of the
transcripts recognises them — they are **not** search terms and must not be
promoted to a table.

The surname *Kehrle* is the worst offender and appears as kerley, kerly, Kelly,
kler, KLE, Keller, querler, querme, quer lei, "quer ler" and "Kherle" (the last
is the channel's own spelling in the title of chapter 13, so it is a real
publication variant, not an ASR error). *Cimbres* becomes simbres, simples,
símbries, síbrides, Cres, sim-s, cimbre and — repeatedly — "singles" and
"besteira"; searching a transcript for "Cimbres" will therefore miss most of
its occurrences, and "simples" is a false friend because it is also an ordinary
Portuguese word. *Ororubá* comes out as "ouro Ubá", "ouro Uruguai", "de Ubá",
"ororubá" and "Serra de ouro bar"; *Xukuru* as xucurus, chucuru and "xucuru do
Ororubá". *Sobral* survives, but Dom Adalberto appears once as "Dom Adalberto
Soares Sobral" and once, in the UNICAP dissertation's own text, as "Dom
Adalberto Palmeira" — a conflation with a later bishop. *Frei Estevão von
Roettger* is rendered Estevam hotger, Estevon de Rottega, "Frei Stevan" and
"frei Estevo". *Adélia* is usually right but appears as "irmã dela", "irmã
Adri", "irmã Diabia" and "Vandéliia" — and "irmã dela" ("her sister") is the
dangerous one, because it is grammatical Portuguese and a regex will not flag
it. *Alagadiço* (the second reported site, near Alagoinha) is heard as
"alagadiço", "o agradeço" and "Jenipapinho"; *Buíque* as "Bíque" and "Buíque";
*Pesqueira* as "pesqueiro" and "besteira". Numbers fare badly too: the parish
church "construída no ano de 1692" is transcribed "no ano de 692", and "maio de
1936" as "Maio de 1976".

Practical consequence: **do not report a zero from these transcripts on a
single spelling.** Any absence claim over this corpus has to sweep the variants
above and pair itself with a positive control.

## Known zeros (verified 2026-08-05, web search via session proxy)

- "11 de fevereiro de 1937" as the final apparition date: appears in search
  snippets but was NOT found in the text of any reachable source checked
  (templariodemaria.com, salvemariaimaculada.com.br fetched and grepped;
  positive control: "6 de agosto de 1936" found in both). Re-checked 2026-08
  against the 2016 UNICAP dissertation, whose full text is in hand and which
  gives **26 de fevereiro de 1937** for the last appearance at Sítio Guarda
  (positive control: "26 de fevereiro de 1937" occurs twice in that file,
  "11 de fevereiro" zero times). Still no source found for the 11 February
  date.
- Any formal 1930s–40s diocesan decree on the apparitions: none located in
  the academic study (RBHR 2023), the UNICAP dissertation (2016), Catholic
  press, or devotional sites reached. The 2023 study states the cult
  consolidated "sem grandes interferências institucionais da Cúria local ou da
  Cúria romana".
- **A named document for the 1930s–40s prohibition, in eighteen devotional
  videos** (~78,000 words, vaulted in the shared archive, swept 2026-08-05):
  zero. Method: the whole corpus was flowed to single-line text and swept for
  `decreto|protocolo|arquivo|ofício|edital|documento` with 300 characters of
  context either side. Every speaker asserts an episcopal prohibition; not one
  names a decree, a protocol number or an archival reference. Positive control
  by the same method: `livro|editora|edição` returns dozens of hits and yielded
  the whole bibliography now in `references[]`, so the sweep worked. What the
  videos *do* name is documents of other kinds — Kehrle's diary, Frei Estevão's
  diary, Adélia's diaries and letters, a 1937 private letter from the bishop to
  Kehrle, a medical certificate by Dr. Lídio Paraíba, and a sanctuary decree
  said to be reproduced in Ione Paiva's book.
- No press coverage of the 1936 events in the *Jornal do Commercio* (Recife) or
  the *Diário de Pernambuco* for August–November 1936 — not this project's
  check but the 2016 UNICAP dissertation's, which states it explicitly and
  invites future work on the point.
